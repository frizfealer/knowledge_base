# Varentropy

* Variance of entropy. It measures the uncertainty across different tokens.
* High entropy with high varentropy vs high entropy with low varentropy
	* [0.12, 0.12, 0.13, 0.13, 0.5] (entropy = 2 (max=2.3), varentropy = 1)
		*   high entropy and high varentropy -> COT
	*  [0.01, 0.13, 0.28, 0.29, 0.29] (entropy = 2, varentropy = 0.365)
		* high varentropy means the uncertainty varies largely across tokens.
		*  high entropy and low varetropy = resample
* Low entropy with high varentropy vs low entropy with low varentropy.
	* [0.01, 0.02, 0.02, 0.41, 0.54] (entropy = 1.3, varentropy=1.3). 
		* -> branch
	* [0.05, 0.05, 0.06, 0.09, 0.75] (entropy = 1.3, varentropy=2.3)
		* -> argmax
	* Looks that low entropy and low varentropy needs branch instead
