---
title: local-currency EM bonds add portfolio volatility
---

**Date time:** 2026-06-14
## Contents
Bonds are usually held to dampen portfolio volatility, but local-currency emerging-market bonds carry unhedged exposure to the issuer's currency. That FX exposure adds variance on top of the bond's own price moves, so a local-currency EM bond can raise portfolio volatility instead of lowering it — the opposite of the role bonds are bought for.
