---
title: unhedged local-currency EM-bond carry overstates real return
---

**Date time:** 2026-06-14T09:33
## Contents
A local-currency EM bond's high yield is not free return — it largely compensates for the risk of holding that currency. The carry can be entirely eaten up when the currency depreciates against your home currency, so the headline yield overstates the real return you actually keep. (e.g. South-African ZAR 10Y bonds yield ~8.5%, but a fall in ZAR against USD erodes that carry for a USD investor.)
